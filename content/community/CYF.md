# Business Analysis Training at CodeYourFuture

## Organisation

[CodeYourFuture](https://codeyourfuture.io/) is a non-profit organisation that supports refugees, asylum seekers and disadvantaged individuals by providing education and mentoring in software development and technology skills.

The organisation works with volunteers from across the technology industry to help students develop practical technical and professional capabilities that improve employability and access to careers in technology.

---

## My Role

As a volunteer contributor, I designed and delivered a dedicated Business Analysis and Requirements Engineering module for software development students participating in the CodeYourFuture programme.

The objective of the module was to introduce students to the role of business analysis within agile software delivery in preparation for interviews and junior roles with an industry partner.

The module was taught in sprints with an emphasis on students shaping their own learning path and presenting their results to the group. Assessment was carried out weekly and at the end of the module. 

---

## Topics Covered

- Introduction to Business Analysis
- Agile software delivery
- Requirements elicitation
- Stakeholder management
- User stories and acceptance criteria
- BPMN and process modelling concepts
- Backlog management
- Collaboration between business and engineering teams
