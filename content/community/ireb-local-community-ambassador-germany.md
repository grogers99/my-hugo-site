# IREB Local Community Ambassador for Germany

## Organisation

[IREB – International Requirements Engineering Board](https://www.ireb.org/) is a global non-profit organisation dedicated to advancing professional standards in Requirements Engineering and related disciplines.

The Local Community initiative forms part of IREB's effort to build stronger practitioner networks, support local engagement and make professional learning more accessible across different regions and communities.

---

## Role and Contributions

As Local Community Ambassador for Germany since January 2024, I support IREB's engagement with Requirements Engineering practitioners in Germany. We organise regular face-to-face meetings around Germany with presentations from experts in the field, as well as online sessions on specific topics or simply as open question and answer sessions with the community ambassadors. 

Our aim is to help requirements engineers to continue developing their knowledge in the field, to expand their professional networks and to encourage a thriving community with an open exchange of ideas and experiences.

The role is particularly important in creating alignment between certification, professional practice and real-world delivery needs. Supporting local communities strengthens the practical relevance of IREB's work and helps ensure that professional standards continue to evolve in response to current industry challenges.

---

## Focus Areas

- strengthening local professional networks
- supporting knowledge exchange and community engagement
- promoting high-quality Requirements Engineering practice
- connecting certification work with practical delivery challenges
- encouraging collaboration between practitioners and educators
