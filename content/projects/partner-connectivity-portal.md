# Partner Connectivity Portal for Global Agritech Supply Chain

## Overview

Led requirements engineering and implementation coordination for a large-scale partner connectivity portal supporting logistics and supply chain processes in global seed production operations.

The solution integrated logistics providers, ERP systems and distributed development teams within a scaled agile delivery model.
