# FTTH Reseller Enablement at Telefónica O2

## Overview

Lead Product Owner within a large-scale FTTH broadband transformation program enabling Telefónica O2 Germany to operate as a reseller of Deutsche Telekom FTTH infrastructure.

The project formed part of Telefónica's Gigabit Champions strategy and involved significant transformation across the OSS/BSS landscape.

---

## Responsibilities

- Lead Product Owner in a Scrum-of-Scrums organisation
- Coordination of 4 frontend and 2 backend agile delivery teams
- Stakeholder management across business and technical domains
- Requirements engineering and backlog management
- User story and epic definition
- Cross-team dependency management
- Workshop facilitation and solution clarification

---

## Key Challenges

- Integration across multiple BSS and provisioning systems
- Complex FTTH provisioning and activation workflows
- Coordination of frontend and backend increments
- Migration toward cloud-native microservices architecture
- Alignment of agile delivery across multiple teams and suppliers

---

## Key Achievements

- Successful delivery of first FTTH product increment ("Connected")
- On-time rollout across multiple agile release cycles
- Migration of critical Deutsche Telekom data interface to cloud infrastructure
- Improved accessibility and security for partner integrations
