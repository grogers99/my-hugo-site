Partner Connectivity Portal for Global Agritech Supply Chain

Overview

Led requirements engineering and implementation coordination for a large-scale partner connectivity portal supporting logistics and supply chain processes in global seed production operations.

The solution integrated logistics providers, ERP systems and distributed development teams within a scaled agile delivery model.

Responsibilities

  • Requirements elicitation and documentation
  • Workshop facilitation across business and IT teams
  • Coordination of distributed BA and testing teams
  • Process modelling and solution analysis
  • Agile delivery support and backlog management
  • Quality and documentation standards

Key Challenges

  • Complex supply chain integration landscape
  • International distributed delivery teams
  • Integration with ERP and logistics providers
  • Standardisation of documentation and processes
  • Alignment between business and technical stakeholders

Key Achievements

  • Successful rollout of partner integration platform
  • Introduction of scaled agile delivery practices
  • Improved collaboration across global teams
  • Acceleration of development and delivery processes
