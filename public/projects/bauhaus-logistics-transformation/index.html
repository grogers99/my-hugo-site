Global Logistics Transformation at Bauhaus

Overview

Product Owner responsible for end-to-end logistics systems supporting international eCommerce operations for a major European retailer.

The role covered warehouse onboarding, transport management, carrier integrations and process standardisation across multiple countries and warehouse hubs.

Responsibilities

  • Managed BA and Support Engineering teams
  • Ownership of roadmap for OMS/WMS/TMS development
  • Coordination of internal and external delivery teams
  • Requirements workshops with international stakeholders
  • Definition and prioritisation of system improvements
  • Support of onboarding initiatives for warehouses, couriers and countries

Key Challenges

  • Coordination across international warehouse hubs
  • Standardisation of logistics processes between countries
  • Complex SAP and messaging integrations
  • Balancing business priorities with technical dependencies
  • Integration of multiple carriers and fulfilment systems

Key Achievements

  • Realignment of 20+ ongoing projects into a coherent roadmap
  • Successful onboarding of new courier partners
  • Delivery of significant operational cost savings
  • Completion of elicitation and solution analysis for partner-country redesign initiative
