Scalix BIM Integration Requirements Engineering

Overview

Freelance requirements engineering engagement supporting the evolution of a structural engineering calculation platform and planned integration with BIM tooling.

The work combines stakeholder management, backlog preparation and solution definition for engineering-focused software products.

Responsibilities

  • Requirements gathering and analysis
  • Stakeholder workshops with engineers and architects
  • User story and epic creation
  • Backlog refinement and prioritisation
  • Sprint preparation and Definition of Ready activities
  • AI-supported prototyping and concept exploration

Key Challenges

  • Translating engineering workflows into software requirements
  • Alignment between calculation engines and BIM platforms
  • Complex technical domain concepts
  • Balancing technical and commercial priorities

Key Achievements

  • Delivery of comprehensive RFP requirements package
  • Preparation of solution concept for Scalix / Axis VM integration
  • Prototype definition for cloud-based engineering project storage
