Contributor, Editor and Review for IREB Magazine

Organisation

The IREB Magazine is a professional publication designed to share insights, case studies, methods and practitioner perspectives related to Requirements Engineering.

It plays an important role in connecting theory with real-world practice across industry sectors and professional communities.

Role and Contributions

I contribute to the IREB Magazine as a writer, editor and reviewer. This work includes helping review and refine submissions, ensuring articles remain relevant, technically sound and useful to practitioners in the field.

I also contribute articles and commentary that connect professional standards, agile delivery practice and real-world transformation experience. The objective is to provide practical value for readers who want to improve their Requirements Engineering skills and apply them in modern, fast-moving delivery environments.

Focus Areas

  • professional writing and editorial review
  • translating practice into clear, accessible guidance
  • connecting theoretical foundations with enterprise delivery
  • supporting knowledge sharing across the RE community
  • improving quality and relevance of published content
