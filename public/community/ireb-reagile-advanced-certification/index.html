Coauthor and Examiner for RE@Agile Advanced Certification

Organisation

The RE@Agile programme was developed by IREB to support Requirements Engineering practitioners working in agile and iterative delivery environments.

RE@Agile aims to combine the discipline and established knowledge of requirements engineering with the insights and goals of agile development. It provides pragmatic solutions for the application of requirements engineering methods in iterative and incremental development. It’s goal is to professionalise requirements engineering practice and provide a certified career path for requirements engineering, product owners and IT professionals.

Role and Contributions

As a member of the RE@Agile Working Group from 2015 to 2026, I was a co-author of the Primer and Advanced Level (later Practitioner and Specialist levels) syllabi and handbooks. I continue to be an examiner for the Advanced Level Certification.
