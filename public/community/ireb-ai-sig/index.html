IREB Special Interest Group for AI Prototyping

Organisation

IREB hosts Special Interest Groups to support focused discussion and exploration of emerging topics relevant to Requirements Engineering and professional practice.

The AI-related work stream explores the intersection between modern AI capabilities and practical requirements work, including opportunities for faster prototyping, analysis support and decision-making assistance.

This SIG work contributes to better understanding of where AI can create meaningful value in Requirements Engineering, while keeping a strong emphasis on quality, traceability and human-centred decision-making.

Role and Contributions

From March 2025, I contributed to the IREB Special Interest Group AI4RE as a reviewer and editor. In 2026 I led a smaller group to investigate the topic of AI-assisted Prototyping as a requirements engineering method.

Focus Areas

  • AI-assisted requirements analysis
  • rapid prototyping of business ideas
  • exploratory concept testing
  • practical integration of AI into business and product workflows
